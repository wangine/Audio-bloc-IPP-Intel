Cockos WDL resampler
Upstream: https://github.com/justinfrankel/WDL
Pinned source: 2b260b915f8ca7cb7859b9c0b7ba7e48b166f0af

Entropy's RendererLive/Wdl.cmake creates a marked source adaptation in the build
directory: a fixed 0.97 low-pass cutoff for near-unity drift and disabled dynamic
ideal-grid selection. Sample and filter types are double. The original WDL
software is by Cockos, not Entropy. This modified integration is not upstream WDL.
